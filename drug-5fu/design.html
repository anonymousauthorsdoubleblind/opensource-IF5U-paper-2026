The same flow in words

  1. Ask one question. Run unchanged on a drug, does the open-source workflow reach one stable structure from rough starts, with an infrared spectrum consistent with the lab?
  2. Pick the drug and its form. 5-fluorouracil, T1 form, 12 atoms. The lab spectrum is of molecules isolated in solid argon at 6 K.
  3. Make three starts. A standard start and two deliberately bent ones, 3.11 and 6.38 eV above the final energy.
  4. Optimize (Module 1). L-BFGS on Skala-1.1 forces (def2-SVP), from each of the three starts.
  5. Check the gate. Do the bent starts end at Run 1's shape? Yes: within 0.0072 Å and 0.51 meV. Run 3 is a geometry result only.
  6. Compute the vibrations once (Module 2). From the Run 2 structure: re-optimize at def2-TZVP and build the Hessian. 30 real frequencies, none imaginary.
  7. Compare, then state the claim. 25 bands, one scale factor fitted on the same bands. The infrared is consistent with the lab spectrum, and nothing more is claimed.

Choices at a glance

Why 5-fluorouracil?

It is a widely used anticancer drug, small enough (12 atoms) for a full Hessian on one cloud GPU, and it has a published, tabulated infrared spectrum of isolated molecules. Its identity was checked: our T1 structure has the same InChIKey as ChEMBL CHEMBL185 and PubChem CID 3385.

Why only the T1 form?

T1 (2,4-diketo) is the form of the clinical drug and the only form observed in the argon-matrix experiment; other forms were below detection there. A clean comparison needs the same form on both sides.

Why bent starts?

Screening pipelines hand over rough shapes from fast tools. We made strained starts on purpose (3.1 and 6.4 eV above the final energy) to mimic that, and asked whether every start reaches the same structure.

Why an argon-matrix spectrum?

No gas-phase infrared spectrum of 5-FU was found. Molecules isolated in solid argon at 6 K are the closest available match to a single molecule. Solid-state (KBr) spectra are shifted by hydrogen bonding and are not a fair check.

Why Module 2 starts from Run 2

The vibrations were computed once, from the structure that Module 1 relaxed from the 3.1 eV start. That keeps the infrared result tied to a bent start. No further runs were made after that, so there is no infrared from the 6.4 eV start.

Why report the scale factor the way we do

One factor (0.9736) was fitted on the same 25 bands it is judged on. We say so, and we report the unscaled errors next to the scaled ones, so a reader can judge both.

Which energies

All energies are electronic energies at fixed nuclei, from one method at a time (Skala-1.1 with the stated basis). Free energies in thermo.json use the harmonic, ideal-gas, rigid-rotor model.

1 · Question and scope

  • Question. Run unchanged on a drug molecule, does the open-source Skala-1.1 workflow take rough, strained starting shapes to one stable structure whose computed infrared (IR) spectrum is consistent with a laboratory spectrum?
  • Molecule. 5-fluorouracil (5-FU), C₄H₃FN₂O₂, 12 atoms, neutral closed-shell singlet, gas-phase model. An anticancer drug, first approved in 1962 (ChEMBL CHEMBL185; PubChem CID 3385; ATC L01BC02).
  • Form. Only the 2,4-diketo tautomer, T1 (5-fluoropyrimidine-2,4(1H,3H)-dione, SMILES O=c1[nH]cc(F)c(=O)[nH]1). It is the form of the clinical drug and the only form detected in the argon-matrix experiment used for comparison.
  • Modules used. Module 1 (geometry optimizer) and Module 2 (Hessian, thermochemistry, IR). Modules 3 (NMR) and 4 (molecular properties) were used for the annulenes only.

2 · Why bent starts

Screening pipelines produce approximate geometries (force-field, semi-empirical or machine-learned). A usable refinement step must relax them to the same minimum regardless of the start. Two strained starts were therefore made on purpose.

3 · Why Module 2 starts from Run 2

Module 2 read the structure that Module 1 had relaxed from the 3.1 eV start, re-optimized it at def2-TZVP (largest force 0.0099 eV/Å, 19 gradient evaluations) and built the finite-difference Hessian (73 force calculations, ±0.01 Å). The hand-off is gated: Module 2 refuses a deformed-start structure unless the same-structure check against Run 1 passed (shape difference below 0.02 Å and energy difference below 10 meV, a rule written before the run). That keeps the infrared result tied to a bent start.

4 · Why an argon-matrix reference

No gas-phase IR spectrum of 5-FU was found. Ivanov et al. (Low Temperature Physics 43, 400, 2017) tabulate 26 fundamentals of 5-FU isolated in argon at 6 K, with integrated intensities and assignments; 25 of them (1800 to 300 cm⁻¹) are compared. Solid-state (KBr) spectra are shifted by hydrogen bonding and were not used. The N–H stretches are not in that table and are not compared.

5 · How the comparison is made

  • Bands are paired by frequency order; the character of the C=O, C6–H out-of-plane and N–H out-of-plane modes was checked.
  • One least-squares scale factor, 0.9736, fitted on the same 25 bands (in-sample).
  • Reported: unscaled mean offset (+21 cm⁻¹), unscaled mean absolute error below and above 1000 cm⁻¹ (10 and 46 cm⁻¹), scaled mean absolute error (11 cm⁻¹), largest scaled error (29 cm⁻¹), rank correlation of intensities (0.86), and the two C=O bands (1764.5 vs 1761.2 and 1740.3 vs 1742.0 cm⁻¹).
  • Everything is recomputed by code/analysis/ir_compare.py; the full tables are on Data tables.

6 · The geometry optimizer (Module 1)

L-BFGS runs in passes on Skala-1.1 forces with the def2-SVP basis. Each step is limited by a step cap, the largest move allowed per step, and the run stops when the largest force is below the target. The three runs needed 23, 64 and 72 gradient evaluations. Every step of every run is listed in Table 3, and the run is drawn on How it ran.
